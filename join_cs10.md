# Joining CS 10 Late – What You Need to Know

## Can I still catch up?  
**Yes!** With effort, you can get up to speed.

## What should I do first?  

1. Read Ed Discussion Post: To join [EdStem, click here.](https://edstem.org/us/join/We446g)
2. Fill out the [Late-add Google Form](https://forms.gle/1uddYXWbg5b283Mf7). After submitting the form, refer to the [Late Add Deadline Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OuvI5zrnotGsDOUV41U_PgF_VPbDg5m6pjEjP7JyN9o/edit?usp=sharing) to find your extended due dates. There are several tabs at the bottom of the tracker, each labeled by week and date (e.g., "Week 2 Wed – 9/2"). Select the one that most closely matches the date you joined CS10 and/or attended your first lecture.

    For example, if you officially enrolled in CS10 on Tuesday, 9/1, you should use the "Week 2 Wed – 9/2" tab to view your updated deadlines. Your new due dates will appear in the "Due Date" column of the sheet.

    **You do not have to receive approval or confirmation from course staff. Just start working directly, following the extended due dates in the Late Add Deadline Tracker.**

    NOTE 1: Gradescope will still show the original due date and mark late submissions as late—this is expected. As long as you submit by your extended due date in the tracker, we'll manually waive that penalty; no need to email us about it.

    NOTE 2: Lecture Quizzes are optional, supplementary practice and will not affect your grade. That said, we strongly encourage late joiners to attempt them anyway. They're a good way to catch up on material you missed.

3. Read Website: Start from the beginning—go through the syllabus, lectures, and assignments in order. The class schedule can be found on our [home page](https://cs10.org{{ site.baseurl }}/).

## Will I be penalized for missed work?  
**No**, you won’t be penalized for assignments due before you joined.

## Will deadlines be adjusted for me?  
**Yes**, assignments based on the week you join and past work will be adjusted.

## Do I still need to take the first exam?  
**Yes**, all students are required to take the first exam: **Quest**. Other exam policies can be found in our [syllabus](https://cs10.org{{ site.baseurl }}/syllabus/#exams)!

## How much time will it take to catch up?  
It depends, but expect to spend extra hours at first.

## Can I get extra help?  
**Yes!** Our Regular and Support OH times can be found on this [calendar](https://cs10.org{{ site.baseurl }}/calendar/).
